FAQs

What launch monitor is best for my home?

For users with the ability to mount the technology to the ceiling, we recommend APOGEE by TruGolf. This ceiling mounted system provides an immersive experience with instant shot display – and no marked clubs or specialty balls.

If you plan to build your indoor golf set up in a smaller footprint, or need flexibility, the LaunchBox by TruGolf is the best fit. LaunchBox measures Club and Ball data without marked clubs or specialty balls. This ground based Launch Monitor is easy to move to take with you to the range.

What launch monitor is best for my business?

The APOGEE Launch Monitor was built with design help from TruGolf indoor golf facility owners. The ceiling mounted system features a laser indicator that shows new users where to put the ball – creating a confusion free customer experience, with no frustrating missed shots. APOGEE’s ability to measure Club and Ball data without marked clubs or specialty balls makes it easy for your customers to get the data they need to improve their game.

I already have a golf simulator. Will MultiSport ARCADE work with it?

MultiSport ARCADE is a separate hardware device from TruGolf. It mounts to the ceiling and can work alongside any Launch Monitor from any Golf Simulator brand.

What data does APOGEE show?

APOGEE Displays:

  • Club Head Speed, Club Path, Club Face Angle, Face to Path, Angle of Attack, Smash Factor, Dynamic Lie,* and Dynamic Loft.*
  • Ball Speed, Launch Angle, Horizontal Launch Direction, Backspin, Side Spin, Carry Distance, Total Distance.
  • Peak Height, Descent Angle, Offline Distance, Total Curvature, Roll Distance, Flight Time Shot Shape, Time to Apex, and Spin Loft.

*Users are required to use Club Stickers to measure Dynamic Loft and Dynamic Lie.

How much space do I need for these launch monitors?

TruGolf Launch Monitors work in most rooms. For commercial facilities, we recommend at least 15’ Width, 10’ Tall, and 20’ Feet. This allows for most users, regardless of height and swing style, to play freely in the golf simulator.

APOGEE is required to be mounted between 9’-10’.
